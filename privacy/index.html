Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Freezie collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. Freezie is run by Cube Kit Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16665144), whose registered office is at 6 Langsett Grove, Harrogate, HG3 2FP. Cube Kit Ltd is the data controller for the app and this website.

Questions? Email support@getfreezie.com.

The short version

  • Other people see your nickname, never your real name or phone number.
  • Listings only show a rough area. Your exact address is only shared with the one person whose offer you accept.
  • We don't sell your information or show you ads.
  • Usage analytics are off unless you turn them on.
  • You can download your data or delete your account in the app at any time.

Automatic checks

To keep Freezie safe, we check listings and messages automatically:

  • Listings: photos and text are checked by an AI service to suggest a title and category and to flag items that aren't allowed. Flagged listings are reviewed by a person.
  • Messages: messages are checked for requests for money, off-app links, contact details, abuse and reselling. Flagged messages trigger a warning and may be reviewed by our team.
  • Holds: if three different people you've dealt with report you within 30 days, your account is put on hold until someone reviews it.

These checks support decisions made by people. You can contact us if you think a decision about you was wrong.

Our legal reasons for using your information

  • To provide the service you've asked for (contract): your account, listings, offers, pickups, messages and Freezie Plus.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping Freezie safe, preventing fraud and abuse, moderation, keeping a one-way fingerprint (hash) of banned phone numbers, and fixing bugs.
  • Consent: usage analytics, push notifications and device location. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app or your phone's settings.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information, for example to respond to the police.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information: until you delete your account.
  • Listing photos: deleted 30 days after a listing ends. Photos that are never used in a listing are deleted automatically.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, listings, offers, messages, ratings and saved searches. Backups are deleted within 30 days.
  • Reports and moderation records: 2 years after the case is closed, so we can deal with repeated problems.
  • Banned phone number fingerprints: 2 years after the ban.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, ask us to limit how we use it, object to how we use it, and take a copy with you. You can download your data and delete your account in the app, under Profile. For anything else, email support@getfreezie.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and our fonts are hosted here rather than loaded from other companies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Children

Freezie is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we'll close it.

Changes

If we make important changes to this policy, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
