Safety, built in

Freezie is for people aged 18 and over. Here's how we help keep handovers easy and safe.

Nicknames, not names

People see a nickname you choose, never your real name.

Doorstep handovers

Leave it out or meet at the door. Nobody needs to come inside.

Report and block

Report an item, a message or a person in two taps. We review reports every day.

Checked chats

Messages asking for money or off-app payment are flagged automatically.

If you ever feel in danger, call 999 first, then tell us in the app.

Support

Answers to the most common questions. Can't find what you need? Email us.

Is everything on Freezie really free?

Yes. Items are always free. Nobody can sell anything on Freezie, and asking for money breaks our rules. The only thing we charge for is Freezie Plus, which lets you collect.

How do I cancel Freezie Plus?

Plus is billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Freezie. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for. Refunds follow Apple's rules.

The item wasn't there when I arrived

Tap "It wasn't there" in the pickup chat. The pickup ends, nobody's reliability score is affected, and the lister is asked to check the listing.

How do I report someone or something?

Use Report on the item, in the chat, or on the person's profile. If anything made you feel unsafe at a pickup, tell us after the pickup and we'll look at it first.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete account. It removes your account, listings, offers and messages. You can also download a copy of your data from Profile first.
